How to read this: "First flag (raw)" is the first alert reported by the production W-Twin output when such a value exists. It is kept separate from the per-row flag vector. "First sustained alert" is taken directly from the production output when available; otherwise it is derived from five consecutive flagged points. Production W-Twin columns are authoritative and are not replaced by the browser-side approximation. If no production W-Twin outputs exist, the browser performs a local demonstration analysis. This gives a directional read on your specific log, not a guaranteed diagnosis.
